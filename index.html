A few caveats worth knowing: 2.1 only counts as replacement where child mortality is low, and the real global figure is closer to 2.2 or 2.3. The rate is a snapshot of one year, so when people put off having children it reads lower than the families those people actually end up with. China's recent numbers are disputed.
